# Key

Nine letters are replaced by figures, and o also by a cross (+). Every other letter is written as itself.

| Letter | a | e | i | o | u | t | n | s | r |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sign | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 or + | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |

The five vowels take 1 to 5 in order. The figures 6 to 9 then go to t, n, s and r, four of the most common
consonants. The writer used 4 and + for o without any clear rule: "H4p2" (hope) and "L+v2" (love), and both in
"1f62974+7" (afternoon). The 5 is written small and open, so it can look like an r, and the cursive g looks like a 9.
