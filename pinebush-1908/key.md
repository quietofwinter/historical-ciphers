# Key

One symbol per letter. The shapes are described in words, since they are hand-drawn. "Times" = occurrences on the card,
not counting the covered word.

| Letter | Symbol | Times |
|---|---|---|
| A | a plain horizontal dash | 5 |
| C | a dash with a dot above and a dot below (like ÷) | 1 |
| E | a small arch (∩) over a dash | 5 |
| F | a cup (∪) with two bars beneath | 3 |
| G | an angle, upright with a foot (⌞) | 3 |
| H | two dashes crossed by a slant stroke (like ≠) | 1 |
| I | a dash with an upright tick at the right end (⊣) | 7 |
| L | a dash with a small circle on it | 3 |
| M | a circle with a cross through it (⊕, sometimes drawn like φ) | 3 |
| N | an arch (∩) | 5 |
| O | a cup (∪) | 6 |
| R | a box (□), sometimes drawn open on one side | 8 |
| S | a hook, a bar with a stroke down at the end (⌐) | 1 |
| T | a box with a cross inside (⊠) | 6 |
| U | a dash with an upright at each end (\|–\|) | 3 |
| V | a circle with a dash through it (⊖) | 1 |
| W | a cross like X, drawn with curved strokes | 2 |
| Y | a dash with three upright bars, like a ladder | 2 |

Not seen on the card: B, D, J, K, P, Q, X, Z. (D and P would be in the covered word.)

Every symbol has the same meaning wherever it occurs. Some shapes are related: A, I, U and Y are all a dash with
0, 1, 2 or 3 uprights; N and O are the same arch turned up and down; R and T are the box plain and crossed; L, M and V are
circles. No rule for the whole alphabet was found, and with 18 letters seen none is needed.
