# Sources

The letter is in private hands. Photographs of the five pages are in `photos/` (full size) and `photos/thumbs/`; they were
published by the dealer and by Klaus Schmeh, credited below.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Dealer's description, transcription and photograph of page 1 | Traces Écrites, ref. 11273, "Passionnante lettre codée de Louis XIV, à son ambassadeur à Rome": https://traces-ecrites.com/document/passionnante-lettre-codee-de-louis-xiv-a-son-ambassadeur-a-rome/ |
| Photographs of all five pages | Klaus Schmeh, "Can you decipher this letter written by Louis XIV?", 31 January 2019: https://scienceblogs.de/klausis-krypto-kolumne/2019/01/31/can-you-decipher-this-letter-written-by-louis-xiv/ |
| Partial transcription, frequency counts, description of related codes | Satoshi Tomokiyo, Cryptiana, "French Ciphers during the Reign of Louis XIV", section (4B): https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/louisxiv.htm#SEC4B |

The cipher is on pages 1 to 3; pages 4 and 5 are plain text. The plain text in `../letter-annotated.txt` follows the dealer's
transcription.

## Readings corrected from the photographs

`../ciphertext.txt` was read from the photographs. It differs from the published transcriptions in these groups:

| Place | Published | Photograph |
|---|---|---|
| p. 1, first cipher line | 433 | 477 |
| p. 1, second line | 823 132 309 493 | 523 172 309 433 |
| p. 1, second and third lines | 403 (twice) | 407 |
| p. 1, fourth line | 204 | 209 |
| p. 2, second line | 458 | 456 |
| p. 2, line beginning 481 | 449 | 459 |
| p. 3, first line | 395 | 396 |
| p. 3, line beginning 30 | 183 | 180 |

The dealer's transcription also leaves out about 100 groups in the middle of page 2 (given by Tomokiyo), and prints the word
"et" where the photograph has a group that looks like 12.

In this hand 7 is written like an open angle and is easy to confuse with 3 and 9; 5 has a long stroke to the left. The four
doubtful figures listed in the main README are all of this kind.
